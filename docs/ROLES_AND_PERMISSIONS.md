# Roles and permissions
Receptionist: register/edit demographics, book/reschedule/cancel appointments, check in, issue initial ticket, requeue; no clinical notes/history. Nurse: assigned queues, triage, vitals, nursing notes, authorized clinical history, clinical priority, transfer and completion. Doctor: assigned queues, vitals, SOAP notes/signing, authorized history, clinical priority, transfer and completion. Service staff: assigned queues, call/recall/skip/requeue, transfer and complete; no general clinical history. Admin: users, roles, departments, services, counters, settings and operational reports; no implicit clinical or reception permissions. Multiple roles allowed when authorized. Device identity: sanitized display only. Enforce department and care relationship scope server-side, deny by default, audit access. Validate exact clinical access and emergency access policy before deployment.

# Queue engine
Daily per-service numbering PREFIX-001, reset by configured local operational date; preserve historical prefix and sequence. Patient PT-######, appointment APT-YYYYMMDD-####, visit V-YYYYMMDD-####. Generate atomically with unique constraints. PostgreSQL transaction locks counter and eligible ticket; idempotency prevents double clicks; after-commit broadcast only. Define stable ordering and prevent starvation without overriding clinical urgency. Proposed settings (not approved): early eligibility 15 min, late grace 15 min, walk-in safeguard 45 min, max recalls 3. Clinical urgency assigned by authorized clinicians; emergencies follow clinic protocol.
Transitions: waiting→called/cancelled; called→serving/skipped/cancelled; skipped→waiting/cancelled; serving→completed/transferred. Recall is event only. Transfer atomically creates next ticket and preserves visit. Record every transition, priority change and recall in append-only events. Counter cannot call another while active. Reconnect via authoritative snapshot; deduplicate broadcasts and serialize audio. Public announcement contains ticket and room only. Define end-of-day handling and requeue ordering before production.

# Requirements v1
Confirmed: single clinic; walk-ins and appointments; receptionist-only registration and booking; optional triage; per-service tickets; display and voice; demographics and clinical notes; Livewire and Blade.
MVP: dashboard, patients, appointments, visits, queues, clinical records, departments/services, counters, staff, displays, reports, settings. Exclude billing, insurance, e-prescribing, pharmacy inventory, laboratory result processing, patient portal, multi-branch and AI diagnosis.
Proposed defaults requiring validation: SOAP notes, vital signs, daily reset in clinic timezone, clinical priority assigned by authorized staff, appointment eligibility and fairness settings, browser speech, Spatie RBAC. Use synthetic data only until compliance approval.
